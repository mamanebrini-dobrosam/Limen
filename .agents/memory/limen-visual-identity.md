---
name: LIMEN visual identity
description: Owner's source-of-truth and symbol-preservation requirements for palette work.
---

Use the owner's unmarked original logo palette as the color and artwork source.
The red annotations on its selection copy only identify approved variants;
they must never enter the artwork or sampled colors. Preserve the original
doorway symbol and the name “limen”; do not regenerate or redesign the mark.
The official logo's original colors and proportions must also remain unchanged.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested the original shape and name to remain,
with only the selected variants offered for room backgrounds.

**How to apply:** For later visual changes, use the original supplied artwork,
not a newly drawn interpretation. Readability adjustments to background colors
are permitted, but do not alter the source logo to achieve them.

The owner rejected a loud application-wide combination of the palette colors.
The main application should be elegant, modern, harmonious and minimalist:
neutral surfaces with carefully chosen palette accents, not all colors at once.
Keep all twelve selected variants as private-room background options, with
clearly readable tables, seats, messages and buttons. Show visual proposals
first; do not apply a new direction to the application until the owner chooses.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected the previous appearance and required
approval before further application changes.

**How to apply:** Explore in isolated prototypes. Treat the room-background
catalog as options for individual rooms, not a mandate to color the whole
application. Preserve functional, QR, chat and security behavior.
